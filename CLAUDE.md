## After every turn

End every reply with a **Next:** line: what the human does next and what you do next. Work it out fresh each time from `git log` and the [Order of work](assets/PLAN.md#order-of-work) in `assets/PLAN.md`. Never write the current step into a file: it goes stale within the hour.

- **Good:** "**Next:** you: run the two-phone check on Render. Me: the join rate limit (P4)."
- **Bad:** a "Where we are" block in this file, or a reply that ends without a next step.
